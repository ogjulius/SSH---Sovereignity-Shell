# Code of Conduct

SSH is built by people who care about their desktops and about each other.
This code applies to every SSH space: the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions and any channel that speaks for the project.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most disagreements are about trade-offs.
- Critique work, not people. Explain *why*, and offer a better path when you can.
- Be patient with newcomers; everyone was new to Wayland once.
- Respect people's time: search before asking, give reproduction steps, follow up.
- Respect privacy: never post someone else's logs, screenshots or personal data
  without their consent.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity,
  background or ability.
- Sexualised language or imagery, threats, or encouraging harm.
- Deliberate disruption, trolling, or sustained off-topic noise.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Using the project to promote copying other projects' work without credit.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and warn, temporarily restrict,
or permanently ban participants who break this code, in proportion to the
harm caused. Report concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub
(open a private security advisory if no other private channel exists, and mark
it as a conduct report). Reports are handled confidentially, and reporters
will not face retaliation.

Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith may be removed from the
project.
